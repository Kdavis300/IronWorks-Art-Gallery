Who I Am And What I Want To Accomplish

I am Gemini Zer0 and freelance graphic designer and character artist. My experiences online have left me wanting a more comfortable place to share art. I don't want to share just my art, I want to celebrate all artist. So I created this social network for artist to connect with likeminded individuals and to feel safe while discussing their passions. I believe the only example of bad art is art that has no effort placed into it. Welcome to IronWorks.
